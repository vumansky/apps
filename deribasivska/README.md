# Deribasivska, autumn — a 20-second film made entirely in code

A travel-marketing spot for Deribasivska Street in Odesa, Ukraine, on a November morning.
Everything is generated by the scripts in this folder: the street model (Blender / Cycles via the
`bpy` Python module), the camera move, the grade, the titles and the music. No external 3D assets,
textures, samples or stock footage are used.

## The shot

One continuous camera move, 480 frames at 24 fps:

| time | what happens |
|------|--------------|
| 0–3 s | Skimming centimetres above wet granite setts and fallen plane leaves, garlands in soft focus |
| 3–8 s | Gliding up the street under the golden canopy, past a café terrace, leaves falling |
| 8–10 s | The camera turns to a sunlit yellow-and-white 19th-century facade through a gap in the trees |
| 10–13 s | Climb through the canopy |
| 13–20 s | Reveal: rooftops, the Opera, the port and the sun rising over the Black Sea; title |

## What is modelled

- **Street**: ~600 m of the pedestrian street; the first 80 m of setts are real geometry
  (about 100 000 individually bevelled stones), the rest a matching procedural material;
  kerbs, slab pavements, round cast-iron tree grates.
- **Facades**: 40 unique procedurally generated buildings in the Odesa idiom — rusticated
  shell-limestone ground floors, stuccoed upper storeys in cream / ochre / salmon / pistachio with
  white trim, pedimented and arched windows, pilasters, quoins, dentilled cornices, balustraded
  parapets, wrought-iron "pot-bellied" balconies, carriage arches with iron gates, shopfronts with
  awnings, mansard roofs and corner turrets.
- **Trees**: five plane-tree variants (mottled bark, branching skeleton, ~9 500 palmate mesh
  leaves each with translucency) instanced ~200 times; 42 000 leaves on the ground; 1 500
  falling leaves animated analytically.
- **Furniture**: twin-lantern cast-iron lamp posts, benches, garlands of bulbs, café terraces,
  planters of chrysanthemums, the City Garden with its rotunda.
- **City**: perimeter blocks with courtyards and 3 400 background trees, a simplified Opera house,
  the park slope, port moles, cranes, ships, the Vorontsov lighthouse and the sea.
- **Light**: physically based sky, low sun almost on the street axis. This is geographically
  motivated: the street runs roughly ESE, which is where the sun rises over the bay in mid-November.

## Result

`final/deribasivska_autumn.mp4` — 1920x1080, 24 fps, 20 s, H.264 + AAC. Stills and the
reference-photo comparison sheets are in the same folder.

## Pipeline

```
python build.py                      # build the scene -> out/scene.blend   (~6 s)
./run_render.sh                      # Cycles, 1280x720 linear EXR + depth  (~3 h on 4 CPU cores)
./run_patch.sh                       # re-render of frames 118-236 with the corrected yellow facade
python music.py out/music.wav        # synthesise the score                 (~10 s)
python make_video.py out/exr out/deribasivska_autumn.mp4 --music out/music.wav
```

- `dz/` — scene modules: `mb.py` mesh builder, `mats.py` procedural materials, `facade.py`
  building generator, `trees.py`, `street.py`, `city.py`, `world.py` sky/sun/render settings,
  `cam.py` camera path.
- `render.py` — renders colour frames (low sample count + OpenImageDenoise, motion blur, depth of
  field) and a separate 1-sample depth pass.
- `post.py` — depth-reprojected temporal accumulation (removes low-sample flicker), distance and
  height haze, screen-space sun shafts, bloom, filmic tone curve, grade.
- `make_video.py` — Lanczos upscale to 1080p, sharpening, grain, titles, H.264/AAC encode.
- `music.py` — original 8-bar piece in D major at 96 bpm; felt piano, string ensemble, solo
  violin line, pizzicato, percussion and reverb are all synthesised with numpy/scipy.
- `tools/fetch_refs.py` — downloads reference photographs from Wikimedia Commons (not committed);
  `tools/compare.py` builds side-by-side sheets of reference photos and renders.

## Honest limitations

- It is a stylised reconstruction, not a survey. Building order, exact house designs and the
  distance to the sea are compressed or invented in the local style; only the general layout
  (pedestrian sett-paved street, tree rows, City Garden on the north side, Opera to the north-east,
  port and bay to the east) follows reality.
- Rendered on a 4-core CPU, so frames are 720p at 7–8 samples per pixel, denoised and upscaled.
- The street is empty of people; background buildings are simple massing with procedural windows.
